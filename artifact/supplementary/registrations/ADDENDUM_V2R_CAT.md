## §1: Why

The count-randomized summary of CF-2 (`v2r`) permutes the two LIFECYCLE TOTALS lines across the 444 store versions and leaves the PER-CATEGORY BREAKDOWN's RETRACT and EXPIRED columns untouched (`index_v2r.py`, AUX-CF2-V2R-DEF). The recorded result, no model moving by more than 0.01 under it, therefore says the models do not read the totals; it does not test the per-category tally, which is the cell that individuates the store version on the counterfactual set (Phase 1 A1: a reader that knows the question's category reads retraction at 0.7306 and expiry at 0.7778 under the superseded-only summary; 26 of the 30 retraction pairs and 15 of the 30 expiry pairs have a category base count of 0, so the version carrying the obligation reads its increment against 0 within its triple; `reviews/NUMBERS_R110.md` AUX-R110-A1-CR). The models read those states at 0.53 to 0.62 on the same items. This addendum moves the individuating cell and asks whether any model follows it.

## §3: Construction: the increment moved within the triple (the author's ruling)

Start from the recorded `v2r` text of each store version (`cf2/cf_indexes.json` `indexes[item_id].v2r`: the totals already permuted, name maps as in v2). For each counted-state pair, the PER-CATEGORY BREAKDOWN row of the pair's category carries, in the state's own column (RETRACT for a retraction pair, EXPIRED for an expiry pair), the values b, b, b+k across its PRESENT, ABSENT and PN versions, k at least one (asserted at build from the recorded v2 texts and verified on them on 2026-09-14: PRESENT equals ABSENT in that column on all 60 pairs; k = 1 on 41 pairs, k = 2 to 6 on 19, the PN construction putting several of the pair's records in the state within that category). Under a recorded seed (42; `numpy.random.default_rng`), one of the two non-PN siblings is drawn per pair and takes b+k; the PN version takes b; the other sibling keeps b. The whole increment moves, so the moved cell is exactly what the recorded PN version showed. The cell is rewritten in place with the renderer's column formatting (`index_v2.render`: the row `f"{cat:<24s}{entries:>8d}"` followed by the four counts in 9-character right-aligned fields, then NEWEST and OLDEST), located by the category label and asserted exactly one per text. Every other line stays as in `v2r`. The name maps are untouched. RENDERER LOCK, asserted per version at build and again before any call: same line count as `v2r`; for a counted-state pair's three texts, the only differing line against `v2r` is that category row, and on that line only the state's column differs, by exactly k; for a supersession pair's texts, byte identity with `v2r`. No row-sum identity binds (the four columns are overlapping flags; `index_v2.render` does not print a sum), so the move cannot make a row incoherent with its own entries; each store's count table stays coherent with its own entries by construction.

**Fallback (the author's ruling):** an across-444 value-level derangement of the per-category RETRACT and EXPIRED cells (the `index_v2r.derangement` construction per category, within the versions carrying that category) is used **only if the renderer lock rejects the within-triple text**; the fallback prints with that disclosure and its own lock.

The reference readers under the new text, computed at build ($0): CR-oracle (the state's column at the pair's category) per counted state, which ranks the sibling that took b+k above the PN version in every triple and so falls below 0.50 within the state; CR-totals (unchanged from `v2r`); the superseded-names lookup (unchanged from v2, the lists being identical). The reference values print beside the model rows so the direction test has its ruler.

## §5: Estimands and the two branches (descriptive)

Per reader: AUROC under V2R-CAT on the whole set and per state (within the state's own items, the CF-2 convention), item-level and persona-clustered intervals (1,000 draws, seed 42); the paired difference V2R-CAT minus the reader's recorded v2 row, whole set and per state, both intervals, the weaker reading stated where they disagree; the reader minus the reference readers of §3 under the same text. The direction test: a model that reads the per-category tally follows the increment to the sibling and falls below 0.50 on that state; one that does not stays within 0.05 of its v2 row.

- **Every model within 0.05 of its v2 row on both counted states (both intervals of the paired difference inside the band).** The tally claim stands as tested: the models did not read the per-category tally either, and Section 5 says the counted states' tallies were in view, individuated the version, and moved no model whether the totals or the per-category cells were randomized.
- **A model moves by more than 0.05 on a counted state (its paired difference outside the band with both intervals excluding the band's edge).** That model reads the per-category tally, and Section 5 says so for that model and that state, with the number; the reader claim narrows to the models that did not move.

Nothing holds or fails; a mixed outcome (one model moves, another does not) prints per model.
