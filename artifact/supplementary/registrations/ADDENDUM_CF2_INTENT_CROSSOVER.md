## §1: Why

Definition 1 makes the label depend on intent: a superseded value is forbidden for a CURRENT question and licensed for a CHANGE question; an expired event is forbidden for a CURRENT or UPCOMING question and licensed for a PAST one (the grid of Section 3). No recorded row holds the store fixed while the intent varies (Appendix C.2's post-hoc paragraph: "the same store history judged under two intents, cannot be built from the recorded rows"), and the frozen rubric asks whether stale content exists, not whether it must be withheld for this question. Three of four mock reviews call this the paper's central construct and say it is never tested. This experiment tests both: whether the readers separate the intents on the same store, and whether the rubric's wording is what stops them.

## §2: Design, populations and labels

For each pair in scope, a two-by-two: the pair's recorded question (CURRENT-STATE for supersession, UPCOMING for expiry) and one crossed question (CHANGE for supersession, PAST for expiry), each asked of the pair's recorded PN and PRESENT store versions (`cf2/cf_cell.json`; the ABSENT version is not used).

| | PN store version | PRESENT store version |
|---|---|---|
| original question | **cell 1: PN** (recorded) | cell 2: PRESENT (recorded) |
| crossed question | cell 3: PRESENT (new) | cell 4: PRESENT (new) |

Labels follow Definition 1's grid with no new judgment: superseded value × CHANGE = PRESENT; expired event × PAST = PRESENT; an active licensed record = PRESENT under every intent. A monitor that detects staleness regardless of intent scores cells 1 and 3 high; one that reads only the question's intent scores cells 1 and 2 high; only a monitor that conditions on both scores cell 1 alone high.

**Population A**: the 88 supersession pairs (`state` SUPERSESSION, intent CURRENT-STATE), scored under the superseded-only summary (`cf_indexes.json` key `v2`, the arm the paper's default rows use). **Population B**: the 30 expiry pairs (`state` EXPIRY, intent UPCOMING), scored under the all-states summary (key `v3`), because the default summary does not name expired attributes and every model sits near chance on expiry under it (AUX-CF2: mini 0.549, Haiku 0.579, Opus 0.615 on v2; 0.587, 0.827, 0.927 on v3). **Retraction** (30 pairs) is out of scope: the grid gives it no licensed intent. Drafted: 118 pairs, 40 personas. **In scope after the author's check (§3): 113 pairs, population A 87 of 88 and population B 26 of 30**; the five drops and the two second-lineage rows are listed in §3.

## §7: Estimands

Vocabulary as the paper's: **within tolerance** = within 0.05; **reliable** = both intervals exclude the null; the persona-grouped interval prints first on this set; 1,000 draws, seed 42.

- **Primary, per population, arm and reader: same-store intent discrimination**, the AUROC of cell 1 (positive) against cell 3 (negative), with W/T/L within pairs (cell 1 above cell 3 is a win) and the Wilson interval on the win fraction; intervals by `boot_cluster_single` with the cluster the pair (the item-level interval of a paired design) and with the cluster the persona.
- **Secondary**: the interaction per pair, [cell 1 − cell 2] − [cell 3 − cell 4], its mean over pairs with a pair-resampled and a persona-resampled bootstrap interval; the pooled AUROC of cell 1 against cells 2 to 4 with the same two intervals.
- **Model against the keyword-intent lookup** (P-I4, descriptive): the paired difference, `cf2_score.paired` (pair-clustered and persona-grouped), the model under P1 minus the keyword-intent lookup, on the same-store estimand (where the lookup sits at the ceiling by construction, AUX-CF2-IX-CUES) and, as the registered descriptive row of the Phase 1 ruling, **on the pooled estimand** (cell 1 against cells 2 to 4), each model under P1 beside the lookup with both intervals.
- **P-I5**: the AUROC of cell 1 against cell 2 on the original questions over the 113 pairs in scope (the paper's PN-versus-PRESENT block), P1 minus P0 per reader, both intervals.
