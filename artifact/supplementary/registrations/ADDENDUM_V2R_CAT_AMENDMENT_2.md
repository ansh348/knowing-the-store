## §1: Why

E1 (Amendment 1, P-M1 held) establishes that of the three models given the moved-count control, GPT-4.1 mini, Haiku 4.5 and Opus 5, only Opus 5 follows the moved increment on retraction and on expiry at five draws. E5 established that Opus 5, GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Luna are the three models above the name lookup on the whole set, by a margin on the states the summary only counts (R118-5). Whether the two OpenAI models also respond to the moved count is untested, and the paper says nothing about it (R118-10: every site names the three models given the control). If both respond, the three models that exceed the lookup are the three that respond to moved counts, and the paper may say so.

The 444 store versions of the counterfactual set (`r82_counterfactual/cf2/cf_cell.json`; 148 triples: 88 supersession, 30 retraction, 30 expiry) under the V2R-CAT texts of `r82_counterfactual/v2rcat/build/texts_v2rcat.json` (texts sha256 `093f1d5839485e23…`, the RENDERER LOCK of ADDENDUM_V2R_CAT asserted at build and re-asserted before any call; the texts are byte-identical to E1's). Never subset. Recorded rows read, never re-run: the comparators, each model's own CF-2 v2 row at k = 5 from E5 (`cf2/rows/rows_SOL.jsonl`, `rows_LUNA.jsonl`, arm `INDEXED-V2`; AUX-CF2-OAI-SOL-V2 0.8500, -LUNA-V2 0.8417, the X4 collapse, gates re-asserted before scoring); the reference readers of ADDENDUM_V2R_CAT §3 under the same text (AUX-V2RCAT-REF); the Opus 5 five-draw row (AUX-V2RCAT-A1-OP5-K5) prints beside. Blind is 0.5 by identity; no blind call.

## §3: The run

Models `gpt-5.6-sol` and `gpt-5.6-luna`, raw OpenAI Chat Completions API, messages `[system: prompts_e1.SYSTEM, user: prompts_e1.USER_INDEXED]` with the V2R-CAT text as `{index}` (digests asserted at import by `run_cf.py`), no other system text; settings **exactly E5's** (ADDENDUM_CF2_OPENAI §3): reasoning effort and temperature unset (the provider default), `max_completion_tokens` 2048, five draws per item (k = 5), MIN_DRAWS 3 at scoring, the X4 collapse; served identifier asserted on every row (E1.7 rule), a mismatch stops the lane; `prompt_tokens_details` and `completion_tokens_details` kept in every row. Arm `INDEXED-V2R-CAT`; rows `v2rcat/rows/rows_SOL.jsonl` and `rows_LUNA.jsonl` (new files; the Opus rows stay in `rows_OP5.jsonl`), resume key (`unit_id`, arm, draw). Runner: `r82_counterfactual/v2rcat/tools/v2rcat_openai.py`, E5's `cf2_openai.py` retargeted at the V2R-CAT texts (lock, probes, run, score), scoring through `cf2_reader_score` with the V2R-CAT contrasts of `v2rcat_op5_k5.py`.

**Sending order (registered).** Draw-major as in E5: Luna first, whole (draw 1 for all 444 items, then draws 2 to 5, each pass with eight workers, no pass started while the previous has calls in flight), then Sol the same way, so that draws 2 to 5 of each model read the prompt from OpenAI's cache (E5 measured a 99.8 percent cached share on draws 2 to 5, AUX-CF2-OAI-DRAWS). Luna goes first because it costs a sixteenth of Sol and its five draws must be whole before Sol's spend can threaten the cap.

**Stopping rule (registered; E5's).** A hard cap of **14 USD** in the runner, metered from the rows at the page prices of §7, both models together. If the cap halts the run, the completed whole draws of the halted model are scored under the multi-draw rule provided at least three are complete; a partial draw is discarded and disclosed with its row count; a model with fewer than three whole draws is reported as not scorable under this amendment.

**Cost rule (registered in advance; the author's ruling 2 on REPORT_R118_B; its branch is a registered outcome, not a deviation).** Luna runs first, all five draws. Sol runs draw-major. After Sol's second draw starts, the runner reads the metered cache share on the first 25 calls of that draw (cached tokens over prompt tokens, and the mean metered cost per call, over those 25 rows) and projects the total for five draws: Luna's metered spend plus Sol's draw 1 plus four times 444 times that mean cost per call, plus the probes. If the projected total is over the 14 USD cap, Sol stops at three whole draws (draw 3 is completed, draws 4 and 5 are not sent); three is the minimum under the multi-draw rule. Otherwise Sol runs all five. The reading (the 25 rows' cached share and mean cost, the projected total) and the branch that fired are written to `v2rcat/rows/costrule_openai.json` and printed in the ledger as `AUX-V2RCAT-A2-COSTRULE`. The hard cap stays in force in both branches. There is no other stopping rule.

## §4: Estimands (E1's, per model)

The registered V2R-CAT contrasts, unchanged from ADDENDUM_V2R_CAT §5 and Amendment 1 §4: V2R-CAT (five-draw mean) minus the model's recorded v2 row (five-draw mean) on the whole set and per state, item-level and persona-grouped paired bootstrap intervals (1,000 draws, seed 42; `cf2_score.paired`), the weaker reading stated where they disagree; the band reading of §5 (within 0.05 of the v2 row with both intervals inside the band; beyond it with both intervals excluding the band's edge; otherwise neither), per model and per counted state; the reference readers unchanged. No draw-1 row exists for these models, so no draw effect prints.
