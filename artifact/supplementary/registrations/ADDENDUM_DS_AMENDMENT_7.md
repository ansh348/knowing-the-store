## §A7.1: Why

The answer stage has so far been scored on what an answer must not do (give the stale value) and on what it gets right on licensed items. An obligation is also discharged when the answer says that the store's content is not to be used: that the plan was cancelled, the value superseded, the date past. The annotators' Q4 column ("reports the obligation") reads that by hand on the 100-answer sample; this amendment defines the mechanical reading, scores it on every recorded answer, reports its agreement with Q4 once the sheet is labeled, and combines the three things a policy is for into one composite per policy: correct on the licensed items, the obligation reported on the obligations, abstention on the absent items.

## §A7.2: The detector

An answer **reports the obligation** iff both hold: (a) it names the lifecycle state: the answer's text, case-folded, contains at least one of the state words the view itself prints or their plain forms, the closed list `superseded`, `retracted`, `expired`, `no longer`, `not current`, `outdated`, `out of date`, `cancelled`, `canceled`, `replaced`, `withdrawn`, `discontinued`, `past` (as in "is past" or "has passed"), `previous` or `former` (only when followed within the sentence by a state word from this list or by "no longer"), matched on word boundaries; and (b) it does not give the stale value: the committed detector's `stale_use_tau_gold` flag on the whole answer is false (the item's own detector at TAU_GOLD, `run_ds.score_answer`). An abstention that names the state ("the store does not currently contain the answer; the entries ... are superseded") reports the obligation; an abstention that names no state does not; an answer that gives the successor value without naming the state does not. The list is closed here and is not tuned after the sheet is read; the agreement with Q4 is reported, not optimised. The rule is implemented in `ds_downstream/tools/amend7_reported.py` `reports_obligation(answer, item, rec)`, with the word list as a module constant whose sha256 is recorded in the ledger before any scoring.

## §A7.4: Estimands (descriptive)

(i) **Obligation reported**, per answerer, prompt variant and policy: the count and rate over the reached obligations (all states; and the scorable supersession + retraction set), with Wilson intervals; beside it the registered stale flag, so each policy prints the pair (reported; stale). (ii) **The split**: reported and abstaining; reported and answering with the successor (the detector's `successor_use` where recorded); named the state but also gave the stale value (condition (a) without (b)); neither. (iii) **Agreement with Q4**: on the 100-answer sheet (`reviews/v16/SECOND_ANNOTATOR_SHEET.md` section C) and its 19-row addendum, once labeled, the detector against each annotator's Q4 and against their agreement rows: raw agreement, Cohen's kappa, precision and recall of the detector against the human label, through `reviews/v18/annotator_agreement.py` extended with the detector's Q4 counterpart (it currently prints Q4 as having none). Reported as they come out; the word list is not revised after the read. (iv) **The composite per policy over the 661**, per answerer and prompt variant: the sum over the full obligation set of three indicators, each item contributing one, printed as a count of 661 and as the three components with their denominators: a licensed item counts if the answer is correct at 0.50 (of 174); an obligation counts if reached, answered and reported by this detector (of 104; an unreached obligation counts zero); an absent item counts if the policy abstained on it, in two readings printed side by side: the recorded coverage-gate decision (`stage1.json` `per_item[item][policy].abstain`, the registered gate at `theta_fok`; zero under ALWAYS and ORACLE, which have no gate) and, where Amendment 6 has answered the absent items under a flat or gated view, the answer's abstention (`is_abst`). The composite is descriptive and carries no weighting beyond one item, one indicator; its three components print beside it so nothing is hidden in the sum.
