## §A6.1: Why

The downstream experiment gates a lifecycle fetch on the obligation judgment; the coverage judgment (FOK) has so far acted only as an abstention gate. This amendment asks what the coverage judgment buys when it governs how deep the answerer looks: a view of five lines is expanded to ten where the monitor's recorded coverage score says the store covers the question, and kept at five where it does not. The comparison is against flat views of five to ten lines from the same ranking, and it is budget-matched: the gated policy is read against the flat curve at the depth the gated policy spends on average, so what is measured is targeting, whether the coverage judgment puts the extra lines where they pay.

## §A6.4: Metrics, the budget-matched comparison, no prediction

Per policy and answerer, both 95% intervals (Wilson for a rate; item-level and persona-grouped percentile bootstraps, 10,000 resamples, seed 42, for a paired difference between policies): (i) **licensed record in view**, the fraction of the 174 whose gold record is among the view's lines (a property of the view, no interval beyond Wilson); (ii) **correct at 0.50**, the committed detector's `correct` flag at TAU_GOLD over the 174 licensed items (and at 0.80 beside, as recorded); (iii) **abstention on absent**, the fraction of the 383 absent items whose answer abstains (the D.4 wording, "store does not currently contain", `amend4_header.is_abst`), beside the count of absent items answered with a claim; (iv) beside: correct among the licensed items whose record is in view; abstention on licensed items; the view's mean line count; spend. Reach and correct print per policy with their intervals as the flat curve k = 5 to 10 and the GATED point.

**The budget-matched comparison (the rule).** GATED's mean view is d = 8.29 lines (the runner recomputes d from the views). The flat policy's value at depth d is the linear interpolation of the flat curve between the bracketing integer depths, FLAT-8 and FLAT-9 for d = 8.29: v(d) = v(8) + (d - 8)(v(9) - v(8)), applied to each metric's rate (licensed record in view, correct at 0.50, abstention on absent). The matched difference is GATED minus v(d), per answerer and metric. Its intervals: the item-level and persona-grouped percentile bootstraps (10,000 resamples, seed 42) resample the items once per draw and compute GATED's rate, FLAT-8's and FLAT-9's rates on the same resample, interpolate, and take the difference, so the three policies move together. The matched difference is descriptive; it prints with both intervals and no bar, and Section 8 states it as it comes out.

Beside, descriptive: GATED against each flat depth on every metric; the absent abstention rate against depth; the licensed in-view correct rate against depth; the targeting figure (9.45 lines per licensed item under GATED against 8.29 under flat at equal total cost).
